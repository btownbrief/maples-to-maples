# Green card seed deck — 12 launch-ready prompts

These are the first 12 green cards, written to get Btown talking. Edit
freely — they're yours. The house style: specific, warm, and locally
knowing. Punch at potholes, construction season, and winter — never at
people or businesses.

`supabase/SETUP.md` has a copy-paste SQL block that loads all 12 into the
queue in this order. You can also add them one at a time from the Editor's
Desk (admin.html), which is how you'll write new ones every week after.

1. The most Burlington thing imaginable
2. What downtown secretly needs more of
3. The real mayor of Church Street
4. The perfect excuse for being late during construction season
5. What Champ does all winter
6. Burlington's official scent
7. The first rule of surviving a Vermont winter
8. What's really at the bottom of that pothole on North Ave
9. Burlington's next official holiday
10. Proof that summer has finally arrived in Btown
11. What Lake Champlain is thinking about at 6 a.m.
12. The most Vermont way to settle an argument
